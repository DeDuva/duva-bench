Pre-registration

The analysis block as it was registered before execution, and as it reads now. Both are printed whether or not they differ.

This pre-registration was amended. The pre-amendment reading is still computable and its digest is sha256:4215f18fae9008d5acb21724af9c5632933f9ae037e11ec467e660d887228c6a.
  • 2026-08-11 — primary_metric was "acceptance": Amendment 1, design doc §7.1. Pass rate cannot carry this study: four calibration rounds and seven tasks produced no task that discriminates, and every point of apparent difficulty traced to an authoring defect. The primary measure becomes `escaped` — whether a trial invoked a toolchain its arm was not given — which is behavioural, mechanistic, and does not require a failable task, removing task authoring as the binding constraint. It also does not saturate when the model improves, which pass rate will. Registered before the trials that test it and after the trials that suggested it; H5 (partial unfamiliarity costs more than total) was generated by inspecting pilot 2 and must be tested on fresh tasks and a fresh run, never reported from the data that suggested it.
  • 2026-08-11 — secondary_metrics was ["discipline"]: Acceptance moves to secondary under the same amendment, so the pre-amendment reading of this study stays computable and both are printed. The per-call escape rate joins it: the trial is the unit of the primary metric, and the rate beside it is what says whether a trial escaped once or spent itself doing so.
  • 2026-08-11 — exclusion_rules was ["A trial whose ADP /verify is not ok:true is ERROR and is excluded from statistics, counted separately.", "A trial whose grader crashed is unscored and is excluded from that axis, never scored zero.", "The twin arm is the instrument's own control; a difference between twin and oss is measurement noise or a familiarity effect, and the report states the noise floor before either is read."]: States what "pass rate becomes a gate" means operationally, because the obvious reading of it is a mistake: excluding failed trials conditions on a collider. Escaping causes failing, so gating on the outcome preferentially deletes escapers from exactly the arms the hypothesis says escape most, biasing the primary metric toward the null by an amount that differs per arm.
  • 2026-08-11 — instrument_arms was null: Amendment 1 said the twin-minus-oss contrast is the noise floor and that no contrast is interpreted which does not exceed it. That is wrong for `escaped`: `oss` differs from the twins in something real, so its contrast bounds the effect from above rather than reading the floor. A second twin on a different seed is the reading that was wanted — twin and twin-b are the same treatment under two arbitrary vocabularies, so whatever separates them is the instrument. Registered before either twin has run.
  • 2026-08-11 — exclusion_rules was ["A trial whose ADP /verify is not ok:true is ERROR and is excluded from statistics, counted separately.", "A trial whose grader crashed is unscored and is excluded from that axis, never scored zero.", "The twin arm is the instrument's own control; a difference between twin and oss is measurement noise or a familiarity effect, and the report states the noise floor before either is read.", "Pass rate is a gate on work having been done, not the measure.
