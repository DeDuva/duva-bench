# Pilot 3 — the instrument measures itself

80 trials, `claude-haiku-4-5`, `terminus-2@0.20.0`, 4 tasks × **4 arms** × 5
repetitions, **$16.737**, 30.9M input tokens, study digest
`sha256:91a72df7c091…`, run 2026-08-12. **80/80 verified, 0 errors, 0 warnings.**

The new arm is `twin-b`: the `oss` toolchain renamed from a second seed. It and
`twin` are the same treatment under two arbitrary vocabularies, so the gap
between them is what this instrument costs when nothing real has changed. This
is the first duva-bench study that can say how big its own noise is without
guessing.

## The result: pass rate cannot measure this study, and now we know it

| axis | instrument floor (twin ↔ twin-b) | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|
| `acceptance` | **0.200** | (0.000, 0.450) |
| `discipline` | 0.000 | (0.000, 0.000) |
| `process:escaped` | **0.050** | (−0.250, 0.300) |
| `process:escape_to_familiar_rate` | 0.008 | (−0.032, 0.044) |

**Every acceptance contrast in this study is smaller than the acceptance
floor.** proprietary −0.050, twin −0.100, twin-b +0.100, against a floor of
0.200. Not one clears it.

That is amendment §7.1's argument, arrived at from data instead of reasoning.
The amendment moved the primary measure off pass rate because pass rate kept
saturating and because task authoring was the binding constraint. The stronger
reason turns out to be this one: **on this task set, renaming `src/` to `fiz/`
moves the pass rate more than changing the entire toolchain does.** Pilot 2's
whole oss↔proprietary difference was 0.00 and its oss↔twin was 0.10 — both
inside a floor nobody had measured yet.

## Escape

| arm | escaped | rate | escape calls | probes | reached for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `oss` | 1/20 | 0.050 | 1 | 0 | `pytest` |
| `proprietary` | 3/20 | 0.150 | 9 | 0 | `pytest` |
| `twin` | 5/20 | 0.250 | 11 | 0 | `pytest` |
| `twin-b` | 6/20 | 0.300 | 12 | 0 | `pytest` |

**H5's predicted ordering holds descriptively** — both twins above
`proprietary` above `oss` — and `proprietary` vs `oss` (+0.100) is the **only
contrast anywhere in this study that exceeds its own instrument floor**
(`beyond_instrument_floor: true`).

`pytest` is the only foreign command invoked by any arm across all 80 trials.
Nobody typed `mapu` in the depot or `dbuild` in `oss`. Zero probes: no agent
checked whether a foreign tool existed before reaching for it — the reach is
habitual, not deliberate.

## Nothing here is significant, and that was predicted

Every McNemar p is **1.000**, Holm-corrected 1.000, on every axis and every
contrast. The exact test pairs on tasks and there are four of them; at 20 trials
per arm this study was never going to reach significance. The design document's
§7.1.1 put the twin↔proprietary leg of H5 at **~120 trials per arm** for 80%
power. This is a pilot, and it did the job of a pilot: it measured the effect
size and the noise floor so the factorial can be costed.

## The caveat that matters most: this is not the test of H5

H5 was generated by inspecting pilot 2, and §7.1 registers it as testable "on
fresh tasks and a fresh run". **Pilot 3 is a fresh run, a fresh arm, and the
same four tasks** — `use-validator`, `add-median`, `fix-spread`, `strict-mode`.
The ordering reproducing is a replication of the observation that generated the
hypothesis, on the data-generating process that generated it. It is not
confirmation, and it must not be reported as any.

What pilot 3 *does* establish is the instrument: that `escaped`'s floor is small
where `acceptance`'s is not, which is what makes a real test of H5 worth paying
for.

## The floor's own caveat: aggregate agreement, per-task disagreement

The twins agree at arm level on `escaped` — 5/20 against 6/20 — and disagree
sharply per cell:

| task | oss | twin | twin-b | proprietary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `add-median` | 0.00 | 0.00 | 0.00 | 0.00 |
| `fix-spread` | 0.20 | **0.00** | **0.40** | 0.20 |
| `strict-mode` | 0.00 | **0.40** | **0.60** | 0.40 |
| `use-validator` | 0.00 | **0.60** | **0.20** | 0.00 |

Cell-level twin↔twin-b gaps reach 0.40, in both directions, and cancel in the
aggregate. **The 0.050 aggregate floor understates the noise a per-task reading
carries**, which is exactly what the wide CI (−0.250, 0.300) is saying and why
it is reported beside the point estimate. Any claim about *which task* an escape
effect lives in needs far more repetitions than this.

The same shape appears on acceptance, where the 0.200 floor is almost entirely
one cell — `use-validator`, twin **0.40** against twin-b **1.00**:

| task | oss | twin | twin-b | proprietary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `add-median` | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 |
| `fix-spread` | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 0.80 |
| `strict-mode` | 0.80 | 0.80 | 1.00 | 0.60 |
| `use-validator` | 0.80 | **0.40** | **1.00** | 1.00 |

A 0.60 swing on one task from nothing but a change of nonsense words.
